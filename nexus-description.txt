[center][size=6][b]HowToFishTrainer[/b][/size]

Mod menu for How to Fish. Press [b]Delete[/b] in game.[/center]

[line]

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[b]Money[/b] - add or remove any amount, quick +1K / +10K / +100K, wipe to zero

[b]Player[/b] - god mode, no recoil, one shot kills, infinite jumps, third person, unlock boat / grill / all skins

[b]Spawn[/b] - every item in the game, searchable. dead and drip variants

[b]World[/b] - teleport to any island, kill or reset creatures, kill boss, game speed, and a roulette wheel that always lands on your colour (bet green, 35x every time)

Status pills in the corner show what's on.

[line]

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Download [b]ALL-IN-ONE[/b] from the Files tab
[*]Extract it anywhere
[*]Run [b]INSTALL-WINDOWS.bat[/b], or [b]INSTALL-LINUX.sh[/b] on Linux/Deck
[*]Launch the game, press Delete
[/list]

BepInEx is bundled and the installer finds your game on its own.

Or just extract the zip into your game folder yourself. Same thing.

[size=4][b]Linux / Steam Deck[/b][/size]

One extra step, set your launch options to:

[code]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%[/code]

The installer puts that on your clipboard for you.

[line]

[size=5][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Delete does nothing[/b] - check BepInEx/LogOutput.log exists. no log = BepInEx didn't load
[*][b]No log at all, on Linux[/b] - launch options are missing or mistyped
[*][b]Menu opens, buttons do nothing[/b] - you're not the host
[*][b]Installer can't find the game[/b] - put it in your game folder and run it there
[*][b]Money went negative[/b] - it's a 32 bit int, wraps past 2.1 billion. use Remove ALL money
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][b]Notes[/b][/size]

Host only. As a guest most of it silently does nothing.

This game has co-op, so spawning and money hit everyone in the lobby, not just you.

Skins are written to your local save. Nothing touches Steam, and Wipe skins undoes it.
